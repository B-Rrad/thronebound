Hero Card Placeholder Gallery

These hero placeholders use the same overall framing language as the realm cards: centered art, restrained border treatment, and a bottom title stack. The gold icon and border establish the shared hero-card identity, and the power description sits directly beneath the portrait area. The roster shown here reflects the locked Greek identities from the hero spec file.

Odysseus

Return one played attack card to your hand.

Achilles

Add one attack card regardless of rank (play with card).

Athena

Cancel one non-trump attack (standalone).

Asclepius

Heal two wounds (any time).

Hermes

Disable trump suit for one round.

Ajax

Auto-defend one attack; attacker discards one random card.

Hades

Defender may use only trump cards (standalone).

Medea

Exchange one card with defender's highest or trump card (start of round).

Argus Panoptes

View opponent's hand (start of round).

Ares

Inflict one wound even if fully defended (end of round, play with card).

Autolycus

The player who played Gollum may redefine trump for one round.

Circe

Name a suit; defender cannot play that suit this round.
